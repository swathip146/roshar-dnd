# Surgebinding Invested Arts — Windrunner, Skybreaker, Dustbringer, Edgedancer, Truthwatcher

Source: `parsed_data/cosmere-5e-the-invested-arts-of-the-cosmere-v2-0/docling.md`, Chapter 1 "Surgebinding Invested Arts" (index lines 123-319, descriptions lines 320-8859).

This catalogue was built by (1) reading the per-order index lists at lines 129-221, then (2) grepping the entire chapter body for every byline containing "Windrunner", "Skybreaker", "Dustbringer", "Edgedancer", or "Truthwatcher" (92 Edgedancer + 113 Truthwatcher raw byline hits, deduplicated to 167 unique art headings — this count is authoritative since it comes from a single grep over the complete body, not sampling), then (3) reading the full body text end-to-end in parallel chunks to extract every field for all 167 headings, then (4) reconciling the per-chunk extractions against the full 167-heading list and back-filling 14 rows (Basic Lightweaving, Brilliant Light, Fortunate Regrowth, Friends, Lightweave All/Copies/Self/Silence, Lightwoven Terror, Message, Rosharbind, Scrying, Weave Fear, Weave Lights) that were present in the source but missing from an earlier assembly pass of this file. All 167 headings are now accounted for (165 in the leveled-arts table below, plus Abrasion cantrip and Progression cantrip, which are Edgedancer/Truthwatcher-relevant but presented once each in the Basic Surges table since they're shared with Windrunner/Skybreaker/Dustbringer). Completeness is provable via the counts in the "count of arts catalogued" section at the end of this file.

**IMPORTANT FINDING — three of the five orders are nearly absent from this chapter.** Windrunner and Skybreaker each have exactly **2 cantrips and zero leveled arts** anywhere in Chapter 1. Dustbringer has exactly **2 cantrips and zero leveled arts**. This was cross-checked two ways: the per-order index (lines 129-144) lists only cantrips for these three orders with no "1st Level" / "2nd Level" headers of their own, and a full-body grep for the order name found no leveled-art bylines at all for any of the three. This is almost certainly because Windrunner/Skybreaker/Dustbringer draw their leveled kits from **Basic Lashing / Full Lashing / Division / Gravitation surge lists that live in a different section of the book** (or are handled as class features rather than "Invested Arts" per se) — not something this extraction missed. Flagging for the audit: if the engine needs full Windrunner/Skybreaker/Dustbringer kits, this chapter alone will NOT provide them; a different chapter or the companion Handbook must be checked.

Edgedancer draws its leveled arts from two surge families: **Abrasion** and **Progression**. Truthwatcher draws its leveled arts from **Illumination** and **Progression**. This is why Edgedancer and Truthwatcher share so many "Progression" entries (both have that surge), and why Truthwatcher overlaps heavily with Lightweaver on "Illumination" entries (both have that surge).

**Investiture Point costs**: no art in this catalogue states a per-art Investiture Point cost in its own entry. Per the project's own Handbook note (`cost_by_art_level`), cost is derived from the art's level, not stated per-art here. No cost numbers have been invented; the "IP cost" column is omitted and Investiture cost should be looked up via the Handbook's level-based cost table.

## Effect types an engine must support

Distinct mechanical shapes observed across these ~170 arts:

1. **Save-or-suffer (no damage split)** — e.g. Charm Person, Hold Person, Command, Sleep, Confusion: target saves or a condition/effect is applied outright, no damage involved.
2. **Save-for-half damage** — the most common shape (Blight, Cone of Cold analogues, Growth-line necrotic blasts, Cognitive Ruin, Surgeburst): fixed dice on fail, half (rounded down) on success.
3. **Save-or-take-damage-only (no half)** — e.g. Abrade, Envenom, Explosive Abrasion, Poison Spray, Deathsurge: full die on fail, zero on success (not half).
4. **Attack roll (ranged or melee) vs. AC, binary hit/miss** — e.g. Abrasive Bolt, Inflict Decay, Vine Whip, Toxic Touch: standard to-hit resolution, not a save.
5. **Attack-roll-with-secondary-effect** — e.g. Abrasive Bolt (grants advantage to next attacker on hit).
6. **Healing (flat + modifier)** — e.g. Regrowth (2d8 + Investiture mod), Dispersed/Plentiful/Ranged Regrowth, Immense Regrowth (multi-target sphere), Heal/Mass Regrowth (flat pool).
7. **Healing (regen-over-time)** — Regenerative Regrowth (1 HP/turn for 1 hour), Emerald Healing/Emerald Sentry (per-trigger healing/damage pool that depletes).
8. **HP-maximum modification** — Aiding Regrowth (+5 max HP), Harm (reduces max HP), Warding Regrowth (death-prevention floor).
9. **Condition application (blinded/charmed/frightened/paralyzed/poisoned/stunned/restrained/prone/incapacitated)** — extremely common; several arts apply conditions with NO damage component at all (Hold Person, Enthrall, Phantasmal Horror's frightened phase).
10. **Ongoing/recurring-save conditions** — condition persists with a save at end of each affected turn to end early (Hold Creature, Confusion, Dominate Person, Bestial Domination) — this is a *loop* construct, not a single resolution.
11. **Charm + creature control (command-based AI override)** — Bestial Domination/Dominate Creature/Dominate Person: grant no-action general commands PLUS an action-cost "total control" mode that needs an actual behavior-override system, not just a status flag.
12. **Movement/forced-movement (push/pull/teleport)** — Grasping Vine (pull 20 ft), Displacement/Disrupture (push/pull), Rosharbind (ground flying creatures), Shadestep/Elsestep/Stormstep (self-teleport), Realmatic Door (long-range teleport with collision damage).
13. **Flight/fall control** — Gravitation's upward Lashing (slow fall), Feather Fall.
14. **Terrain creation/modification** — Entangle, Growth, Thorn Growth, Wall of Thorns, Cultivate's difficult terrain, Cohesion-line stone-shaping (Stoneward, out of scope but same shape). Requires a "battlefield hazard zone" concept: persistent area with entry/turn-start triggers.
15. **Zone/aura effects centered on caster, follows caster** — Life Barrier, Radius of Healing/Hope/Life/Power/Restoration, Essence Armor: an aura that moves with the caster and applies passive/triggerable benefits to creatures inside a radius.
16. **Illusion / sensory fabrication** — Silent Lightweaving, Complex Lightweaving, Programmed/Recorded Lightweaving, Illusory Terrain, Project Image, Illusory Twin: needs an "illusion object" abstraction with a disbelieve-check (Investigation vs. Invested save DC) separate from any save-based resolution.
17. **Divination / information-revealing** — Detect Intention, Detect Poison and Disease, Espy, Scrying, Locate Creature/Object/Animals-or-Plants, Identify, Future Sight: no combat resolution at all — pure GM-mediated information grants.
18. **Buff (advantage/expertise/bonus dice grants)** — Guidance/Resistance (bonus d6 to an already-rolled check/save), Empower (expertise grant), Cultivate (bonus d4 on future rolls), Haste (extra action + AC + advantage).
19. **Debuff (disadvantage/penalty dice)** — Vex (subtract d4 from rolls), Regress (disadvantage on one ability), Weakening Ray (halved Strength-based damage).
20. **Resistance/immunity grants** — Protection from Essence/Poison/Investiture, Quartzskin, Essence Armor, Cognitive Fortitude, Protected Cognition.
21. **Dispel / counter-magic (level-check contest)** — Dispel Investiture, Counter-Invest: roll d20 + ability modifier vs. DC 10 + target Art's level, NOT a saving throw at all — a distinct "ability-check-vs-DC" resolution shape that isn't attack/save/damage.
22. **Reaction-triggered effects** — Astral Barbs, Strike of Intent, Guidance, Resistance, Feather Fall, Counter-Invest, Recall, Shield-analogues: fire off an existing roll's trigger (attack roll succeeded / save just rolled / creature took damage / creature is falling), some allowing "use after roll but before result known."
23. **Resurrection / death-reversal with escalating DC / time windows** — Resurrection, Revivify, Perfect Regrowth: multi-step state check (time elapsed, prior-resurrection-count DC, exhaustion penalty) — needs its own resolution pipeline, not a simple save.
24. **Disease/poison application with player-chosen sub-effects and staged-failure tracking** — Contagion: a 3-successes/3-failures ladder resolved over multiple turns before "locking in" one of five disease effects, more complex than a simple condition timer.
25. **Multi-effect "menu" arts (caster picks sub-effect per cast)** — Enhancing Growth (6 buffs to choose from), Regress (4 debuffs to choose from), Wrath of Cultivation (4 terrain-conditional effects simultaneously).
26. **Summon/conjure (creature control, out-of-scope example noted for context)** — Conjure Castweaving (Lightweaver-only, not in my scope, but the node type is relevant): CR-budgeted summon with shared initiative and GM-adjudicated autonomy limits.
27. **Utility/object interaction (no creature target)** — Mending, Identify (on objects), Locate Object, Purify Food and Drink, Illuminate Traps.

### Node types the project's `maneuver_executor.py` interpreter would need but likely doesn't have (per CLAUDE.md's stated node types: target/save/damage/attack/roll/ieffect2)

- **A "check-vs-DC" node distinct from `save`** — Dispel Investiture / Counter-Invest resolve via an ability check (d20 + modifier, no proficiency) against `10 + Art level`, not a saving throw. If `save` assumes a standard ability-save proficiency model, this needs its own node or a `save` variant that omits proficiency and uses a different DC formula.
- **A "recurring condition with escape save" loop construct** — many arts (Hold Person, Dominate Person, Confusion) require a save at the end of every affected turn until success, decoupled from the original cast. This is a persistent-effect object with its own trigger, not a one-shot `ieffect2` application.
- **An "aura/zone bound to caster position" node** — Life Barrier / Radius-of-* arts need a moving area-of-effect that re-evaluates "who's inside" every tick, distinct from a fixed battlefield hazard (`Entangle`, `Wall of Thorns`).
- **A "depleting-pool" node** — Emerald Healing/Emerald Sentry, Radiant Aura's material cost, Prismatic Weaving/Sleep's HP-pool-consumption-in-ascending-order mechanic: a shared numeric pool that depletes across multiple applications/targets until exhausted.
- **A "creature-control override" node** — Dominate/Bestial Domination's "total and precise control" mode is a temporary AI-behavior takeover, materially different from a `condition` flag; the executor needs some way to hand a turn's decision-making to the caster.
- **An "illusion object with disbelieve check" node** — illusions (11+ arts) need a persistent fake-object/sound with a separate Investigation-vs-DC check gate, distinct from `save`.
- **A "healing" node** — not explicitly listed among target/save/damage/attack/roll/ieffect2; if `damage` is signed/bidirectional this may already cover it, but healing-over-time (Regenerative Regrowth) and pool-splitting heals (Immense/Mass Regrowth "distribute as you choose") need explicit support.
- **A "reaction-timing" node allowing 'after roll, before result known' insertion** — Guidance, Resistance, Astral Barbs, Strike of Intent all require inserting a bonus die into an already-in-flight roll; this is a timing/hook concern more than an effect-shape concern, but the interpreter needs a hook point mid-resolution, not just pre- or post-resolution.
- **A "multi-turn staged failure tracker" node** — Contagion's 3-success/3-failure disease ladder doesn't fit a simple duration timer.
- **A "menu/choice" node for caster-selected sub-effects at cast time** — Enhancing Growth, Regress, Programmed Lightweaving (gem-type-based duration) all need the caster to pick a branch that changes subsequent mechanics, not just flavor.

If `ieffect2` is already a generic "apply arbitrary structured effect" node, several of the above (conditions, resistances, buffs/debuffs) may already be expressible as data within it — but the **check-vs-DC (non-save) resolution**, **recurring-save loops**, **caster-centered moving auras**, and **depleting pools** look like genuine gaps in a target/save/damage/attack/roll/ieffect2 vocabulary and would need new node types or significant extension of existing ones.
