# Cosmere RPG (Brotherwise/Dragonsteel) — Surgebinding, Radiant, and Setting Mechanics

**Scope**: This catalogues the standalone **Cosmere RPG** ("Stormlight" line) — a
d20-based system that is **not** D&D 5e and shares no mechanical chassis with
it (no Armor Class, no saving throws, no proficiency bonus, no spell slots, no
hit dice — verified absent from every source below). It is a **different,
newer game system** from the 5e-based *Cosmere 5e Radiant's Handbook*
catalogued in `docs/mechanics/COSMERE_MECHANICS.md`. The two systems cover the
same Roshar fiction with entirely different rules; this document does not
import any 5e assumption, and nothing here should be merged into
`COSMERE_MECHANICS.md`.

**Sources read (exhaustively)**:
- `parsed_data/sl015-stormlight-starterrules-digital/docling.md` (2,434 lines) — the Stormlight Starter Rules, PRIMARY source. Full heading map produced via `grep -n "^#"`; every heading from part 1 through the GM/adversary chapter at the end was opened and read.
- `parsed_data/cs007-gmrules-overview/docling.md` (142 lines) — GM-facing "quick overview of key distinctions," full text read.
- `parsed_data/cs006-player-quick-reference/docling.md` (129 lines) — player quick reference (actions, reactions, conditions, injury table), full text read.
- All 20 pregenerated Level-1 character sheets under `parsed_data/sl019-*/docling.md`, plus `sl019-introduction` (the "Stormlight Quick Start Characters" menu/table document). Every sheet was opened; the ones with any Investiture/Singer-form/Shardblade content (`agent-worldhopper`, `agent-undercover-workform`, `envoy-keeper-of-songs`, `leader-warform-commander`, `warrior-warpair-partner`, `warrior-alethi-duelist`, `ghostblood-operative`, `leader-azish-vizier`, `scholar-stormwarden`) were read in full.
- Cross-referenced against `docs/mechanics/COSMERE_MECHANICS.md` (the 5e Handbook catalogue) for the comparison sections below.

---

## Headline answer up front

**No. These books do not supersede the missing *Invested Arts of the Cosmere*.**
They defer even more explicitly than the 5e Handbook did. See the dedicated
section below for the full evidence; the short version: **0 of the 10 surges
have a specified cost, action type, and effect anywhere in these three rules
documents or the 20 character sheets.** The Starter Rules' own GM section for
adversaries states outright, of the surges: *"For ease of use, stat blocks
offer simplified versions of the surges from chapter 6 of the Stormlight
Handbook... if you want the flexibility of the full surge rules from the
Stormlight Handbook, feel free to use them!"* (`sl015:2353`) — i.e., the
**Stormlight Handbook**, not the Starter Rules, holds the surge rules, and
even that referenced book is described only by chapter number, never quoted or
included here. No file in this corpus is the Stormlight Handbook.

**Verdict on Investiture economy**: These books use a **third resource pool** (Investiture, alongside Health and Focus) that is explicitly analogous to Focus in structure ("works much like your focus does," `sl015:587`), refilled via Stormlight (spheres/gems) and the Empowered condition. This is a **different economy** from both the 5e Handbook's Investiture Points (level-scaled spell-point costs) and its Lashing Dice (Windrunner/Skybreaker/Dustbringer-specific expendable dice pools, `COSMERE_MECHANICS.md:118-120`). Neither Lashing Dice nor any exact IP-cost table appears anywhere in the Cosmere RPG corpus — the *mechanism* by which Investiture is spent on surges is never specified (see §2).

## 2. Surgebinding — the 10 surges

**Direct answer: none of the 10 surges (Adhesion, Gravitation, Division, Abrasion, Progression, Illumination, Transformation, Transportation, Cohesion, Tension) has a specified cost, action type, or effect anywhere in this corpus.** A full-corpus grep for all ten surge names across all three rules documents and all 21 `sl019-*` files returned only 3 incidental hits, none of which define the surge:

| Surge named | Source (file:line) | What is actually said (verbatim) | Mechanically specified? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gravitation | `sl015:1561` | "However, if you want to fly, you'll need a separate flying rate (granted by effects such as the Gravitation surge)." | **No** — only says Gravitation is *a* source of a flying rate; no cost, DC, range, or the rate itself. |
| Division | `sl015:1665` | Used purely as a worked example for how area-of-effect origin points work: "if you target a large area with the Division surge, only one square of that area needs to be next to your square." | **No** — the example is about the generic area-effect placement rule, not about what Division does, costs, or targets. |
| Transportation | `sl015:1372` | Reactive Strike reaction text excludes "a character who transports themself with Transportation or who otherwise moves instantaneously" from being targetable. | **No** — only establishes that Transportation grants instant movement immune to opportunity attacks; no cost, range, or action type. |
| Adhesion, Abrasion, Progression, Illumination, Transformation, Cohesion, Tension | — | **Zero occurrences** of these 7 surge names anywhere in `sl015`, `cs006`, `cs007`, or any of the 21 `sl019-*` files. | **Not found at all.** |

Every other surge reference in the corpus is generic/collective, never per-surge:
- `sl015:1042` — "Different weapons, **surges**, and other effects deal different damage types." (generic)
- `sl015:1240` — "actions granted by your talents or other effects (such as the Breathe Stormlight action available to Radiants)." (names an action, not a surge)
- `sl015:1376` — attacks "can be weapon attacks... or even attacks that use the powers of the **surges**." (generic)
- `sl015:1461, 1507` — "Many **surges** allow you to make [area] attacks" / "Some **surges** and other abilities can affect... a large physical area." (generic, no named surge, no numbers)
- `sl015:1810` — "some abilities, such as **surges**, generally don't require a skill test." (generic)
- `sl015:2351-2353` (GM adversary stat-block rules) — **the single most explicit deferral in the whole corpus**: *"Surge Skills. If an adversary can wield Investiture, their stat block also lists their surge skills, along with the number of ranks they have in that skill. For ease of use, stat blocks offer simplified versions of the surges from chapter 6 of the Stormlight Handbook. The Investiture cost appears in parentheses after that feature or action's name. (However, if you want the flexibility of the full surge rules from the Stormlight Handbook, feel free to use them!)."* This confirms that even the sample "Spear Infantry" stat block (`sl015:2299-2320`, a Tier 1 Minion with Investiture: 0) carries no surge — no adversary stat block in this corpus actually has Investiture > 0 or a surge skill listed, so there is no worked example of the "simplified surge" format either.

**No surge appears as a `##` heading, in a "chapter 6" excerpt, or in any table.** The document plainly assumes the reader has the Stormlight Handbook in hand; the Starter Rules text at `sl015:174` explicitly separates itself from that book: *"Stormlight Handbook. This book contains the full rules for playing the game, such as how to create characters, roll dice, and play out scenes."* Character creation itself (and by extension surge selection at Radiant-path talent picks) is stated to require that separate handbook (`sl015:402`: "For the full character creation experience, pick up the Stormlight Handbook.").

## 8. Adversaries/NPCs

Fully specified, GM-facing system (`sl015:2217-2379`), read in full:

- **Stat block anatomy**: Title (species/function/name) → Details line ("Tier 2 Boss – Medium Humanoid": Tier for difficulty, Role, Size [Small 2.5ft / Medium 5ft / Large 10ft / Huge 15ft / Gargantuan 20ft+], Type [flavor only]) → attributes/defenses (same 6 attributes + 3 defenses as PCs) → Health/Focus/Investiture (numbers shown are simultaneously current AND max; a health range in parentheses, e.g. "60 (48-72)," lets the GM reweight difficulty) → Deflect value with a parenthetical source name (e.g., "chain") → Skills by category (Physical/Cognitive/Spiritual, only ranked skills listed) → Surge Skills (only if the adversary "can wield Investiture" — none of the sample stat blocks in this corpus have any) → Languages → Features (passive, no action cost) → Actions/Attacks/Reactions (pre-calculated modifiers, e.g. "Attack +11," so the GM never derives them live).
- **Roles**: **Minion** ("attacks can't critically hit, and they're immediately defeated when they suffer an injury" — i.e., one-hit-hazard mooks), **Rival** (standard, no special rule), **Boss** (can take both a Fast turn AND a Slow turn each round; can spend 1 focus after an enemy's turn to take an extra action/reaction; can spend 1 focus on their own turn to remove a condition from themselves).
- **Worked example**: "Spear Infantry" (Tier 1 Minion – Medium Humanoid) — full stat line given (`sl015:2299-2320`): Str 2/Def 14, Int 1/Def 12, Wil 1, Awa 2/Def 13, Pre 1; Health 14 (11-17); Focus 3; Investiture 0 (×3, one per Realm column — confirms Investiture is tracked per-character as a single pool, shown redundantly across the 3-column layout, not as 3 separate sub-pools); Deflect 2 (chain); Movement 25ft; Senses 10ft (sight); skills Athletics +4, Heavy Weaponry +4, Light Weaponry +3, Discipline +2, Intimidation +3, Perception +4; Features: Minion, Martial Drill (auto-Brace at scene start if shielded and not Surprised), Military Tactics (1/round, spend 1 extra focus to Aid/Reactive-Strike without spending an action); Actions: Strike (Shortspear, Attack +3, reach 5ft, Graze 4/1d8 keen, Hit 7/1d8+3 keen +extra 1d8 keen if target Prone), Strike (Shortbow, Attack +3, range 80/320ft, Graze 3/1d6 keen, Hit 6/1d6+3 keen), Shield Bash (Athletics test vs. Physical defense, knocks Prone on success).
- **Damage notation**: every adversary attack gives both a flat number and a dice formula in parentheses (e.g., "8 (1d4 + 6) keen damage") so the GM can pick fast-flat or roll-for-drama at will (`sl015` under "Damage," end of GM section).
- **No adversary in this corpus has Investiture > 0**, so the "simplified surge" format promised at `sl015:2351-2353` is never actually demonstrated with a worked example anywhere in these sources.

No 5e-equivalent adversary/monster stat-block format was cross-checked here (out of scope — that lives in `SRD_EQUIPMENT_MONSTERS_DM.md`, a different catalogue).

## Does this supersede the missing *Invested Arts of the Cosmere* book?

**No — it does not.** Verdict, with the full evidentiary basis:

- **All 10 surges (Adhesion, Gravitation, Division, Abrasion, Progression, Illumination, Transformation, Transportation, Cohesion, Tension): 0 fully specified, 0 partially specified with a real mechanic, 10 unspecified.** The 3 surges that are even *named* in running text (Gravitation, Division, Transportation — see §2 table) are used only as **incidental illustrative examples** for unrelated generic rules (flying-rate sourcing, area-effect origin-point placement, and a Reactive-Strike targeting exclusion, respectively). None of the three gets a cost, an action-type icon, a range, a save/defense targeted, or a damage/effect formula. The other 7 surges (Adhesion, Abrasion, Progression, Illumination, Transformation, Cohesion, Tension) have **zero occurrences** anywhere in the 2,705 combined lines of `sl015` + `cs006` + `cs007`, and zero occurrences across all 21 `sl019-*` character-sheet files.
- **The corpus explicitly and repeatedly names its own missing dependency: the Stormlight Handbook.** Unlike the 5e Handbook (which pointed to a wholly separate, differently-titled book, *The Invested Arts of the Cosmere*), the Cosmere RPG Starter Rules defer to a **same-line, same-publisher sequel book** — "the Stormlight Handbook" — cited by name **13 times** (`sl015:174, 402, 589, 695, 732, 918, 926, 1112, 1427, 1804, 1810, 2223, 2407`) plus once more implicitly via "chapter 6" at the single most important line for this audit, `sl015:2353`: *"stat blocks offer simplified versions of the surges from chapter 6 of the Stormlight Handbook."* This is a **direct structural analogue** of the 5e Handbook's *"See The Invested Arts of the Cosmere"* deferral pattern (`COSMERE_MECHANICS.md:32-40`) — same shape of gap, different missing book's name.
- **Shardplate mechanics are also explicitly deferred**, matching the 5e Handbook's own deferral to *Invested Items Collection* (`COSMERE_MECHANICS.md:656`). Neither system's available text specifies Shardplate's AC/deflect bonus, HP, or donning-time-to-benefit ratio in the documents actually present in this repo.
- **The character sheets — the strongest evidence available, since they are filled-in, playtested outputs — confirm the gap rather than closing it.** All 20 pregenerated Level-1 characters have Investiture fixed at 0. Not one is a Radiant. Not one has a surge listed as an action, talent, or ability. The one archetype whose very name gestures at Shard-items ("Warrior (Shardbearer)") ships with a mundane Greatsword and flavor text about *borrowing* someone else's Shardplate, not a specified Shardblade of its own. This is consistent with — not contradictory to — the rules text's own admission that character creation and surge assignment require the (absent) Stormlight Handbook (`sl015:402`).
- **The only "surge mechanics" present anywhere are meta-level, not object-level**: the GM section tells you *that* adversary stat blocks would list "surge skills" with an "Investiture cost in parentheses" *if* an adversary had any (`sl015:2351-2395`) — a description of a **format**, not a single filled-in instance of that format for any surge.

**Conclusion**: the user's hypothesis is **not supported**. These Starter/Quick-Reference/GM-Overview books are a *lower tier product* in the same product line as the still-needed Stormlight Handbook, deliberately scoped to onboard new players with pregenerated characters and to run early, low-complexity, non-Radiant sessions. They do not contain, and do not claim to contain, full Surgebinding rules. The missing-Invested-Arts problem recorded in `COSMERE_MECHANICS.md` is **not resolved** by this corpus — it is *restated*, under a different book title, once more.
